CITIS AI Academy

Computational Thinking, Coding & Artificial Intelligence for K–12

Think. Build. Command.

Preparing students for the AI-powered world

The CITIS AI Academy is a comprehensive K–12 program designed to develop the skills children need to thrive in an increasingly digital and AI-driven world.

Developed in technical collaboration with CODMOS, South Korea, the program brings together Computational Thinking, Coding Foundations and AI Fluency into one structured learning pathway. Rather than treating coding and Artificial Intelligence as disconnected technology subjects, the Academy develops students progressively—from learning how to think and solve problems, to building with code, to understanding and responsibly using AI.

THINK with Computational ThinkingBUILD with CodingCOMMAND with AI

Why CITIS AI Academy?

From Digital Users to Digital Creators

Today's children are growing up surrounded by technology and Artificial Intelligence. However, simply using digital devices does not necessarily develop digital literacy, computational thinking or AI fluency.

Students need to understand how technology works, how problems can be broken down, how solutions can be designed and how AI can be used intelligently and responsibly.

The CITIS AI Academy addresses this need through a structured, age-appropriate and progressive learning journey that develops logical reasoning, problem-solving, pattern recognition, algorithmic thinking, coding and AI capabilities.

Technical Collaboration with CODMOS, South Korea

Bringing a Global AI-Era Learning Framework to Indian Schools

CITIS AI Academy is offered in technical collaboration with CODMOS, South Korea, bringing a structured international approach to K–12 Computational Thinking, Coding and AI education to institutions in India.

CODMOS is designed as an AI-era learning system for K–12 institutions, connecting computational and mathematical thinking, coding foundations and AI fluency through a single learning pathway.

The CODMOS curriculum is developed in South Korea and is presented for Indian institutions as an Indian classroom-ready, NEP 2020-aligned program.

CODMOS brings the technology and curriculum framework.

CITIS brings the institutional implementation, academic integration and India-focused education ecosystem.

The CITIS AI Academy Learning Framework

THINK → BUILD → COMMAND

01 — THINK

Computational Thinking

Students first learn how to think before they learn how to code.

Computational Thinking helps students break complex problems into smaller parts, identify patterns, understand relationships, develop logical reasoning and create step-by-step solutions.

These capabilities are transferable across Mathematics, Science, ICT and project-based learning.

Students develop:

  • Logical reasoning
  • Problem decomposition
  • Pattern recognition
  • Abstraction
  • Algorithmic thinking
  • Problem-solving
  • Computational and mathematical thinking

02 — BUILD

Coding Foundations

Once students learn to structure their thinking, they learn to convert ideas into working solutions.

Coding is introduced progressively through icon/block-based programming, interactive missions, games and problem-solving activities, moving towards more advanced programming concepts and Python pathways.

Students learn to create, test, debug and improve their solutions.

Students develop:

  • Programming logic
  • Algorithms
  • Sequential thinking
  • Loops
  • Conditions
  • Variables
  • Events
  • Functions
  • Data and analysis
  • Game development
  • Python foundations
  • Debugging and problem-solving

03 — COMMAND

AI Fluency

The final stage is not simply learning about AI, but learning how to understand, question, use and apply AI responsibly.

Students are introduced to age-appropriate AI concepts including recognition, classification, generative AI and AI-based problem-solving. They also explore issues such as bias, privacy and responsibility.

The objective is to help students use AI with judgement rather than dependency.

Students develop:

  • AI literacy
  • Understanding of AI
  • AI recognition and classification
  • Generative AI awareness
  • AI problem-solving
  • AI ethics
  • Responsible AI use
  • Understanding of bias and privacy
  • Ability to question AI outputs

A Progressive K–12 Learning Journey

The Academy follows a grade-banded progression, allowing students to build capabilities continuously rather than repeating the same introductory coding experience every year.

Foundation / Introductory

Students begin with computational thinking diagnostics, spatial and arithmetic puzzles, icon-based coding and basic AI recognition activities.

Foundation

Students progress into sequences, loops, conditionals, data collection, game-making and introductory AI concepts.

Advanced

Students explore automation, simulation, variables, events, AI learning principles and STEAM + AI projects.

Mastery

Students move towards Python foundations, debugging, AI classification projects, generative-AI problem solving and responsible AI use.

Learning Through Missions, Challenges & Projects

Make Learning an Experience

CITIS AI Academy moves beyond traditional lectures and worksheets.

Students learn through interactive missions, puzzles, coding challenges, games, projects and problem-solving activities. This approach allows students to experiment, make mistakes, debug their solutions and learn through iteration.

The program is designed to make learning engaging while keeping student progress measurable.

Learn → Experiment → Create → Debug → Improve → Apply

AI & Coding Projects

The Academy encourages students to move from learning concepts to creating with technology.

Depending on age and level, students can work on:

  • Coding challenges
  • Interactive games
  • Computational thinking puzzles
  • Data projects
  • Python programs
  • AI classification activities
  • AI-based problem-solving projects
  • STEAM + AI projects
  • Generative AI problem-solving activities

The progression is designed to take students from understanding technology to creating with technology.

Responsible AI Education

AI Literacy Must Include AI Responsibility

As AI becomes increasingly accessible, students need to understand not only what AI can do, but also its limitations and responsibilities.

The Academy introduces students to questions such as:

Can we always trust an AI output?

What happens when AI makes a mistake?

Can AI be biased?

How should personal information be protected?

Who is responsible for decisions made with AI?

Students are introduced to concepts such as AI ethics, privacy, bias and responsibility, helping them develop the judgement necessary to use AI responsibly.

Designed for Indian Schools

International Framework. Indian Classroom.

CITIS AI Academy is designed to support the requirements of Indian schools and the changing direction of school education.

The program aligns with the broader emphasis of NEP 2020 and NCF 2023 on competency-based, experiential, multidisciplinary and skill-oriented learning. The source material also notes the introduction of a CBSE Computational Thinking and Artificial Intelligence curriculum framework for Classes III–VIII from Session 2026–27.

This creates an opportunity for schools to establish a structured Computational Thinking and AI pathway rather than introducing isolated coding or AI activities.
