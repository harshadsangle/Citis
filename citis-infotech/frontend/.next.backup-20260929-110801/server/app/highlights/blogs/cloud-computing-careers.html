Cloud Computing Careers – Reasons for a Career in Cloud Computing and Top Cloud Computing Jobs

The term cloud computing, although as old as two and a half decades, came into use only in 2006, when Google and Amazon began using it.

CITIS InfoTech Editorial7 April 20216 min read

Overview

The term cloud computing, although as old as two and a half decades, came into use only in 2006, when Google and Amazon began using it.

Since then, cloud has only grown. It has graduated into a separate career stream with several lucrative opportunities, from a mere technology that it was in the yester decade.

In 2018, the Economic Times published a report stating that India will see over one million cloud computing job roles by 2022.

Now, one million, by no means, is a small number. It only shows that cloud computing will grow even further, thereby making it a career that aspiring tech professionals must not miss.

Besides, the salary of an entry-level cloud professional is around five to seven lacs. It is higher than the salary that a conventional software professional draws – somewhere approximately three to five lacs.

So, the growth of cloud computing, coupled with its significance in the business world, and the career opportunities that it manufactures every year, give you every reason to become a cloud expert.

In this view, this blog discusses four reasons you must pursue a career in cloud computing and some of the top cloud computing jobs around.

4 Reasons to Work in Cloud Computing

Cloud computing is in high demand. Businesses are increasingly upgrading to cloud owing to its techno-commercial benefits. So, as against the traditional software graduate, you stand a better chance of winning the job and making it big in the software industry with cloud skills by your side. Here are four reasons why working in cloud computing is a prudent career decision to make in the days to come.

The Cloud Boom!

Cloud computing is one of the most extensively discussed technologies in the tech-space. Global enterprises look at adopting cloud as a transformation in their IT environment. Cloud is reshaping enterprise IT infrastructure, making private data centers redundant. So, as a cloud computing professional, you’ve got plenty of opportunities in the years to come, and hence, you must go for it!

Cloud is in High Demand

This one, you’ve already seen in the beginning. Let alone the world, India is going to generate millions of cloud computing jobs by 2022. So, if you start now, by the time you step out, you’ll be a part of a growing and in-demand technology and career option.

Range of Career Options

Cloud proficiency opens access to a range of cloud career options, including cloud architect, cloud administrator, and many others. Besides, you can work with the cloud while working as a computer systems analyst, network architect, etc.

Other job roles can entail sending code to the cloud to execute tasks, deploying critical applications on cloud, etc. Such positions include database administrator, computer support expert, information security analyst, computer programmer, web developer, etc.

Further, you may climb the ladder to work at the leadership positions within the cloud computing space. As the cloud vertical head, your role will cover an array of sales, marketing, technical, strategic, and human resources functions, and creating an efficient cloud environment in the company.

You Qualify for Better Positions

Cloud computing is developing technology and career option. There’s always a place and scope for qualified professionals and innovators in the cloud space.

So, as a cloud expert, you stand a better potential of moving up the ranks and working at significant, and decision-making positions. Excellent salary, chance to innovate, and the possibility to work across decisive roles – is an ideal career any different from this?

Top 5 Cloud Computing Careers

Now that you’ve got four good reasons to build a career in cloud computing take a look at five of the significant cloud computing careers.

Cloud Security Architect

As a Cloud security architect, you play a significant role in your company. You engage in developing new and innovative security architectures to keep the data deployed into different cloud and cloud/hybrid systems safe. So, in a way, you contribute to cloud architecture and the security vision and strategy of different types of cloud applications, including PaaS, SaaS, and IaaS.

Cloud Security Architect is a critical position. Hence, companies choose tech professionals with over five years of experience as security architects, and with cloud platforms like AWS.

DevOps Engineer

DevOps engineers are qualified and expert tech professionals. They work towards streamlining the development and integration of various cloud-based solutions.

Besides programming, as a DevOps engineer, you must have project management and system analytics skill, as your job profile will also involve leading a team of software engineers, overlooking operations, and evaluating the efficiency of automation tools.

Cloud Security Engineer

Cloud security engineers look after the security aspect of various cloud platforms, along with analyzing the existing structures, and developing security methods, etc.

Besides, their job profile also involves conducting risk assessment through threat simulations, giving security recommendations, and developing sustainable cloud-based programs.

To become a cloud security engineer, you must possess excellent coding and scripting skills. But, experience with Windows, and Linux, along with expertise at scripting languages such as Python, and comprehensive knowledge of AWS can make you a stronger and a more capable candidate.

Cloud Reliability Engineer

As a cloud reliability engineer, your routine tasks involve monitoring, maintaining, patching, and optimizing cloud platforms. Besides, your profile also includes incident response, and hence, a comprehensive understanding of disaster recovery and redundancy processes also proves useful.

Experience with AWS, Azure, GCP, Open Stack, and an understanding of ITSM, DevOps, IT Security, and ITIL, along with monitoring frameworks come in as an added advantage.

Cloud Consultant

To qualify as a cloud consultant, you must have strong and proven experience in IT, along with a degree in computer science. Besides technical expertise, you should also have excellent communication skills to leverage them for the benefit of the business.

As a cloud consultant, you take up technical research and evaluation based on your company's needs. You also make cloud-based recommendations depending on the result of your assessment.

Conclusion

Cloud is a continually evolving technology and holds a bright future for everyone aspiring to pursue a career as a cloud professional. So, if you, too, are among them, get going right away and secure your position amidst highly competitive, yet prospering cloud computing space.
