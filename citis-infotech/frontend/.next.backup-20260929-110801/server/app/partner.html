Partner With Us

Build the Future of Education Together

Partner with CITIS to create new opportunities in education, skills and emerging technologies.

CITIS Infotech believes that meaningful education transformation is best achieved through collaboration. We invite educational institutions, universities, schools, corporates, training organizations, entrepreneurs, technology companies, industry bodies and professionals to partner with us and create new opportunities in education, skills and emerging technologies.

Whether you are looking to establish a CITIS-enabled education centre, introduce future-ready programs in your institution, offer technology and skill development programs, or explore a strategic business collaboration, we provide multiple partnership models that can be customised to suit the partner's objectives, capabilities and market.

Franchise & Centre Partnership

Build a CITIS Learning Ecosystem in Your Region

CITIS offers opportunities for eligible entrepreneurs, education professionals and organizations to establish CITIS-enabled learning and skill development centres.

Depending on the partnership model, centres can offer programs across AI and emerging technologies, vocational education, skill development, certifications, coding, STEM, professional education and career-oriented programs.

Partners benefit from access to CITIS's academic framework, curriculum, technology ecosystem, branding, training, operational guidance and program support, subject to the applicable partnership terms and eligibility requirements.

Potential Franchise / Centre Models

CITIS Skill Development Centre

Focused on vocational education, technology skills, certifications and employability programs.

CITIS AI Academy

Focused on AI literacy, AI skills, emerging technologies and future-ready learning.

CITIS STEM & Innovation Centre

Focused on STEM education, coding, digital making, science learning and innovation.

CITIS Technology Centre

Focused on advanced technology programs, professional certifications and industry-oriented learning.

Partnership models can be structured based on geography, infrastructure, target learners, programs and the partner's capabilities.

Industry & Corporate Collaboration

Organizations can partner with CITIS to develop technology, skills and workforce initiatives aligned with industry requirements.

Collaboration opportunities may include corporate training, AI adoption and literacy, workforce upskilling, certification programs, industry projects, internships, technology initiatives, research and Centres of Excellence.

We are also open to working with industry organizations to bring real-world projects, mentoring and workplace exposure into academic programs.

Strategic Partnerships

We welcome strategic partnerships with organizations that can contribute technology, expertise, infrastructure, content, market access, industry networks or other capabilities to the CITIS ecosystem.

Strategic partnerships can be explored across:

Education × Technology × AI × Skills × Industry × Innovation

Our objective is to create partnerships where both organizations bring complementary strengths and jointly create scalable solutions.
