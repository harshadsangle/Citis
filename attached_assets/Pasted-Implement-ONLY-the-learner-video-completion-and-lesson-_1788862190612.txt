Implement ONLY the learner video completion and lesson-locking behavior.

Do not change unrelated LMS functionality, APIs, database structure, authentication, navigation, or course content.

VIDEO RULES:

* If a lesson contains a video, the learner must watch the video until it reaches 100% completion before the lesson can be marked as Done.
* Do not allow "Mark as Done" before the video is fully watched.
* Track video playback progress reliably.
* If the learner seeks/skips forward, do not count skipped portions as watched.
* Once the video reaches 100%, enable "Mark as Done".
* After the lesson is marked Done, unlock the Next Lesson/Next Chapter option.
* Before completion, Next Lesson/Next Chapter must remain unavailable/hidden.
* Preserve progress if the learner leaves and returns to the lesson.
* Make the locked state visually clear with a professional message explaining what is required.

CONTENT PROTECTION:

* Disable video download controls.
* Disable right-click/context menu on the video/player where practical.
* Prevent exposing obvious direct download controls or download buttons.
* Prevent common save/download actions where technically possible.
* Add appropriate browser-level protections against casual copying/saving.
* Do NOT claim that screenshots or screen recording can be completely prevented in a normal web browser; implement reasonable deterrence only.
* Do not break normal video playback.

IMPORTANT:

* Apply this specifically to video lessons.
* Non-video lessons should continue using the existing completion behavior.
* Keep the existing CITIS premium UI style.
* Make the locked/unlocked states attractive and intuitive.
* Do not add unnecessary tabs.
