Cisco Certified Support Technician
Networking
Objectives: CCST Networking
The successful candidate has the foundational knowledge and skills necessary to 
demonstrate how networks operate, including the devices, media, and protocols 
that enable network communication. This test will be an entry point into the Cisco 
certification program. The next certification in the pathway is Cisco Certified Network 
Associate.
This is a certification for foundational network technicians, networking students, interns, 
etc. The exam targets secondary and immediate post-secondary students, and IT and 
Networking professionals. The successful candidates are qualified work-ready network 
technicians and customer support technicians, students, and interns with at least 150 
hours of instruction and hands-on experience.
