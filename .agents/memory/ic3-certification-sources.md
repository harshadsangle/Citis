Treat IC3 Fast Track as a digital-literacy readiness assessment, not as an IC3 certification or a badge-awarding outcome. The IC3 Digital Literacy badging page provides the official IC3 Digital Certification badge and explains Credly sharing; GS6 separately awards a Master Certification and Master Badge only after all three GS6 exams are passed.

**Why:** Certiport presents four related IC3 offerings with different outcomes: GS6 and GS5 certification paths, IC3 Spark certification for younger learners, and Fast Track assessment. Collapsing them into one generic credential is misleading.

**How to apply:** Keep the four offerings as separate content records, link badge artwork and badging explanations to Certiport/Credly, and state the GS6 Master outcome only on the GS6 record.
